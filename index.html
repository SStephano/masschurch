What we're about

Faith. People. Purpose.

Church is more than a building or a weekly service. It is people walking through life together, growing in faith, and showing up for one another. MASS Church is being built around Jesus, authentic community, and a shared purpose in the city.

01 / Our foundation

Faith.

Getting to know Jesus, asking honest questions, and discovering a faith that matters in everyday life.

02 / Our community

People.

Building genuine relationships, opening the door wide, and creating a place where people feel welcome and known.

03 / Our calling

Purpose.

Living beyond ourselves by serving others, loving our city, and making a meaningful impact together.
